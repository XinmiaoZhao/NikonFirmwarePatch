# v0.5.0 验证范围 / Validation coverage

## 简体中文

输入为用户自行取得的原厂 Z8 C 3.11，SHA-256 `d67413c2ede6b9ce8887175f20700bd9fbad2a973a4820968c45503d15d80d96`。四组全选包已在机身上完成照片与菜单测试。之后修正了 2.39:1 跟踪框的坐标、C15/C30 的 Fn 尺寸显示和 C60/C120 的无损选项，修正后的全选包 06.63.p0（SHA-256 `1ccb5e89…`）由 Git 源码编译和用工具包默认构建（环境中没有编译器）都逐字节复现，并已在机身上复测这三项及相关功能（见下表的照片裁切和 RAW 预拍）。README 示例的 04.07.p0（`e084a43b…`）与 06.63.p0 解码后只有 16 个字节不同，全部是版本号及随之变化的校验：目录中的模块文件名、固件版本页的指令、两处内部身份、EG 模块 CRC 和包体 CRC；包头的 SHA-1 随之变化。两条构建路径对两个版本号的结果相同。旧版本的证据见 [v0.4.0](validation-v0.4.0.md)、[v0.3.1](validation-v0.3.1.md) 和 [v0.2.0](validation-v0.2.0.md)。

## English

The input is the original Z8 C 3.11 firmware obtained by the user, SHA-256 `d67413c2ede6b9ce8887175f20700bd9fbad2a973a4820968c45503d15d80d96`. The all-group package completed photo and menu tests on the camera. The 2.39:1 tracking-box coordinates, the C15/C30 Fn size display and the C60/C120 lossless option were corrected afterwards; compiling the Git source and running the toolkit's default build without a compiler both reproduce the corrected all-group package 06.63.p0 (SHA-256 `1ccb5e89…`) byte for byte, and the camera retested the three corrections and related functions with it (see Still crops and RAW pre-capture below). The README example 04.07.p0 (`e084a43b…`) and 06.63.p0 differ in only 16 decoded bytes, all of them the version and the checks that follow from it: the module file name in the directory, the firmware-version page instructions, two internal identity strings, the EG module CRC and the body CRC; the header SHA-1 changes with them. Both build paths give the same result for both versions. Evidence for earlier releases is in [v0.4.0](validation-v0.4.0.md), [v0.3.1](validation-v0.3.1.md) and [v0.2.0](validation-v0.2.0.md).

- **Builds.** Each of the 15 nonempty selections is built from the original firmware, packaged and independently unpacked, both with explicit parameters and by detection, then checked for the boundaries and checksums of the five stock modules, every untouched region and the refusal of wrong selections. Source compilation and the prebuilt units produce identical bytes for every selection, and the toolkit ZIP on its own gives the same result.
- **Cold start.** All 15 selections start from a cold boot and show their own version on the firmware version page: the all-group package on the camera, the other 14 in the emulator.
- **Bounded execution.** The shared entry points of the all-group package run under CPU emulation for every case of the added icon lookups, menu conditions and touch keys, together with 88 N-RAW 3:2 entry/mode/proxy checks, 432 SDR HEIF file records, the high-speed HEIF disable and JPEG fallback (54 menu states and 864 shutter conditions), 54 RAW recording page states, the Adobe RGB JPEG ICC writer, the seven-item Fn strip drawing and the pixel-shift key map and return paths. 2.39:1 keeps the processing parameters of the camera-tested version across 960 dispatch cases and 90 full calculations.
- **Emulator.** From a cold boot the all-group package navigates the photo menu, the pixel-shift left-key return, selecting 2.39:1 and restoring FX, selecting each of the seven image areas in the i menu, selecting SDR HEIF and the round trip with the H.265 bitrate page, and the RAW recording page disabling and restoring lossless at C15.
- **Still crops.** 2.39:1 was shot as RAW only, RAW+HEIF, RAW+JPEG fine★ and HEIF at L/M/S; 65:24 as JPEG+NEF and SDR/HLG HEIF at L/M/S. All file sizes match the menu, and every 65:24 HEIF main image and preview decoded in full. 4:3, 1:1, 16:9 and DX HEIFs have the correct sizes. Holding Fn from FX and from 65:24 opened the stock image-area strip, the dial moved the highlight and releasing Fn closed it; selecting and cancelling in the i menu worked. With 06.63.p0, the 2.39:1 tracking, wide-area, subject-detection and touch AF boxes display correctly; with single-point AF moved to the four corners, the focus point shown in playback and the position recorded in the file stay inside the 2.39:1 frame, and the box has the FX size.
- **SDR HEIF and JPEG.** `.HIF` files are 10-bit HEVC coded with BT.601, match same-scene JPEGs and open in macOS Preview, Windows 11 Photos, iPhone Photos and the Xiaomi gallery; interval and time-lapse shooting passed. macOS used the Nikon ICC profile embedded in Adobe RGB JPEGs, which also removed the colour shift in the Xiaomi gallery. C modes switched compressed photos to JPEG, and HEIF returned in ordinary shooting.
- **RAW pre-capture.** C15 in FX recorded 5 history and 15 burst frames, all HE★ with 8280×5520 RAW samples, at about 14.96 frames/s; the ordinary NEFs before and after were lossless. C30 in DX with RAW only and HE★ selected directly recorded 9 history and 30 burst frames with 5408×3608 RAW samples at about 29.9 frames/s; all 39 decoded in full. A RAW+JPEG retest with 06.63.p0 recorded 5 history frames at C15 and 9 at C30, with HE★ NEFs and L fine JPEGs; in both modes the Fn size stays at L with M/S greyed out, also after a power off/on and after removing the battery. Back in single-frame shooting the NEF returned to lossless and L/M/S could be changed again. C60/C120 bursts saved JPEG only (5392×3592 and 4128×2752) with 18 and 36 history frames, as in the stock firmware.
- **N-RAW 3:2.** The all-group package recorded 4.1K 3:2 at 30p for 3 min 05 s without a break (5,556 frames), plus one clip each at 60p, 100p and 120p, all 4128×2752. Each clip's frame count matches its timecode track and every frame has the same size; the 1080p proxies have the same frame rate and count as the clips and decode in full. In-camera playback and warm restarts worked. Development builds recorded 4.1K 3:2 at 24/25/30p and 50p, with a same-scene stock comparison at 24p supporting the oversampling and REDline decoding 4128×2752 with the 8.3K field of view; earlier builds recorded 4.1K 3:2 at 120/100/60p and 8.3K 3:2 at 30/25/24p.
- **Pixel shift.** A four-shot group merged in the camera by the release candidate is byte-identical to the NEFX v0.4.0 merged from the same group. Groups shot in a cropped area or as HE★ are refused, as designed. The new page accepts touches on the MENU/OK bar.
- **Checked on the camera.** sRGB HIF colour is the same in playback right after shooting and after a restart; touching File format works; desqueeze displays correctly at the edges of full-height areas, in the small viewfinder size and with subject-detection boxes; the ProRes stars refresh in each menu and in playback, and the ProRes 422 help title is correct; Log displays correctly after switching menu banks and after a power cycle.
- **Unchanged from v0.4.0.** Log recording, the ProRes and H.265 grades, RAW pre-capture NEF writing and the pixel-shift merge engine keep the v0.4.0 implementation; see [v0.4.0 validation](validation-v0.4.0.md).
